## Built here, not sourced

The cows, bulls, calves, pigs, sheep and chickens are generated in code
(`src/world/critters.js`) — no farm-animal models existed in any of the source
packs. The hospital is likewise assembled from repainted fantasy-town modules,
since the packs contain no medical assets at all.
